Class Features — Barbarian Level 7
Lv1 Rage ▾
Bonus Action. 4 uses per Long Rest. While active: +2 damage on STR attacks, Resistance to Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing, Advantage on STR checks and saves, cannot Concentrate or cast spells. Lasts until end of next turn; extended by attacking, forcing a save, or taking a Bonus Action. Ends early if you don Heavy Armor or become Incapacitated (or Unconscious).
Lv1 Unarmored Defense ▾
While not wearing armor, AC = 10 + DEX modifier + CON modifier. Icarus: 10 + 3 + 3 = 16 AC. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.
Lv1 Weapon Mastery (3 weapons) ▾
Choose 3 Simple or Martial Melee weapons to apply mastery properties. Change choices on Long Rest.
Greataxe — Cleave: On hit, make a free attack against a second creature within 5ft of the first, using same attack/damage rolls (no bonus damage). Only once per attack action.
Handaxe — Nick: When you use Light property on your turn, the extra attack from Nick doesn't require a Bonus Action.
Dagger — Nick: Same as above.
Lv2 Danger Sense ▾
Advantage on Dexterity saving throws unless you have the Incapacitated condition. ▲ ADV on DEX saves
Lv2 Reckless Attack ▾
When making your first attack on your turn, choose to attack recklessly. Gain Advantage on attack rolls using Strength until the start of your next turn. Attack rolls against you also have Advantage during that time.
Lv3 Primal Knowledge ▾
Gain proficiency in one additional skill: Perception (chosen). Additionally, while Rage is active, Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival checks can be made as STR checks.
Lv3 Frenzy (Path of the Berserker) ▾
If you use Reckless Attack while your Rage is active, deal extra damage to the first target you hit on your turn with a Strength-based attack. Roll a number of d6s equal to your Rage Damage bonus (+2) and add them together. Icarus: +2d6 extra damage. Same damage type as weapon.
Lv5 Extra Attack ▾
Attack twice instead of once when you take the Attack action.
Lv5 Fast Movement ▾
Speed increases by 10 feet while not wearing Heavy Armor. Icarus: 25ft base + 10ft = 35ft.
Lv6 Mindless Rage (Path of the Berserker) ▾
Immunity to the Charmed and Frightened conditions while your Rage is active. If Charmed or Frightened when you enter Rage, the condition ends on you.
Lv7 Feral Instinct ▾
Your instincts are honed: Advantage on Initiative rolls. Stacks with Alert feat's +Prof bonus. ▲ ADV on Init
Lv7 Instinctive Pounce ▾
As part of the Bonus Action you take to enter your Rage, you can move up to half your Speed. Icarus: move up to 17ft as part of Rage entry.
Feat Alert — Initiative Proficiency + Swap ▾
Initiative Proficiency: Add your Proficiency Bonus (+3) to Initiative rolls. Icarus total Initiative: +3(DEX) +3(Prof) = +6, with Advantage from Feral Instinct.

Initiative Swap: Immediately after rolling Initiative, swap your result with one willing ally's. Cannot swap if either has Incapacitated condition.
Corvian Species Traits
◈ CORVIAN
Avian humanoids descended from aarakocra, twisted by curse and exile. They bear fused wing-arms like short cloaks, hooked vulture-like beaks, razor talons, and black feathers with blue or purple sheen. Medium size, 6–7ft tall, 100–140 lbs. Their society prizes memory, secrets, and grudges above all else.
Ability Score Increase Intelligence +2; Charisma or Dexterity +1 (chosen: Dexterity).
Size Medium. 6–7 ft tall, 100–140 lbs. Spindly frame.
Speed Base walking speed 25ft (25ft base + 10ft Fast Movement = 35ft for Icarus).
Clumsy Flight Flying speed 15ft. At end of turn, fall if still aloft and can't stop the fall. Cannot use while wearing Medium or Heavy armor.
Darkvision 60ft. Dim light as bright light; darkness as dim light. Can't discern color in darkness.
Memory for Lore Proficiency in the History skill.
Never Forget, Never Forgive Photographic memory — cannot forget anything for at least 1 year. Always recognize faces/voices heard in past year (even disguised, DC Arcana to detect). If a creature dealt damage to you, you remember their face/voice forever and no magic can erase it. Advantage on checks to uncover disguises of creatures you've seen before. ▲ ADV
Paranoia You cannot be surprised while conscious.
One Eye Open Fully aware of surroundings while unconscious or asleep — even when put to sleep by magic. Advantage on saves to resist magical sleep. ▲ ADV
Talons Proficient unarmed strikes. Deal 1d4 Slashing damage on a hit.
Languages Common, Aarakocra, Auran.
